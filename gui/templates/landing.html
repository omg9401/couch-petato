Our Story

Built for pets
who refuse to settle.

Couch Petato started with a simple frustration — every pet bed looked like an afterthought. Cheap fabric, wobbly legs, and zero soul. We knew our pets deserved better.

Everything is handmade in our UAE workshop, on demand. When you configure a piece, we build it from scratch — your size, your colour, your wood finish. That's not a promise, it's just how we work.

We're a small team obsessed with craft, quality, and making things that belong in your home — not hidden under it.

— The Couch Petato Team
